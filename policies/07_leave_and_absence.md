# Leave and Absence Policy

## Types of leave
Northstar distinguishes ordinary PTO from other forms of leave, including sickness absence, family-related leave and other protected or statutory categories.

## Reporting absence
Employees who cannot work because of illness should follow the absence reporting process and notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible. A PTO request should not be used to replace a sickness reporting process where the absence is sickness-related.

## Supporting information
Some leave types may require documentation or HR review. The assistant should not request sensitive medical details beyond what is required by the process.

## Return to work
Managers may conduct a return-to-work check after certain absences. The purpose is to support the employee and confirm any appropriate workplace arrangements.

## Escalation
Questions involving protected leave, complex medical circumstances or legal rights should be escalated to HR rather than answered with unsupported assumptions.
