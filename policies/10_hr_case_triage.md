# HR Case Triage and Escalation Procedure

## Purpose
This procedure helps route HR questions to the appropriate process while avoiding unsupported advice.

## Direct policy questions
Questions that can be answered from the policy corpus may be answered with citations and a clear distinction between policy facts and recommendations.

## Missing evidence
If the relevant policy evidence cannot be found, Xena should say that the corpus does not support a reliable answer and recommend HR review.

## Sensitive matters
Reports involving harassment, discrimination, retaliation, serious conflict, safeguarding, legal claims or protected leave should be escalated. Xena may summarise the information provided by the user but should not investigate or decide an outcome.

## Mock actions
A mock HR ticket may be created only when the user explicitly requests it. The action must be clearly labelled as simulated and must not update a real HR system.

## Auditability
For every workflow, the system should retain an operational trace containing selected tools, tool arguments, outputs, retrieved policy sources and the final answer basis. Hidden model reasoning must never be exposed.
