# Business Expenses and Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose
Employees may claim reasonable business expenses incurred for authorised work activities. Personal purchases are not automatically reimbursable.

## Approval
Where practical, employees should obtain approval before significant or unusual expenditure. Travel and equipment purchases may require additional approval depending on value and department.

## Evidence
Claims should include an itemised receipt, date, business purpose and any required project or cost-centre information. Missing evidence can delay or prevent reimbursement.

## Home office equipment
Home-office purchases are reimbursable only where the relevant equipment category and approval requirements are satisfied. A laptop or chair should not be described as reimbursable solely because it is used for work.

## Travel
Reasonable travel costs incurred for approved business travel may be reimbursed. Employees should use approved booking channels where required and follow the applicable travel limits.

## Safety and exceptions
Xena should distinguish policy eligibility from an actual finance approval. Unusual or high-value expenses should be escalated rather than treated as automatically approved.
