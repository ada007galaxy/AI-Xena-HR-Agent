# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Before the start date
New starters should receive required documentation, system access instructions and an onboarding schedule. Managers are responsible for ensuring role-specific onboarding activities are planned.

## First week
The first week should cover security training, company policies, role expectations, equipment, communication channels and introductions to relevant colleagues.

## Equipment
Equipment issued to an employee should be recorded and used in accordance with the information security policy. Employees should report missing or damaged equipment promptly.

## Access
System access should follow least-privilege principles. Access not required for the role should not be granted simply for convenience.

## Completion
Onboarding is complete when mandatory training, required access, equipment handover and manager check-ins have been completed. Xena can produce a checklist from policy evidence but should not claim a task is complete without supporting data.
