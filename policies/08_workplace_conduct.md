# Workplace Conduct and Respect Policy

## Expected conduct
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and partners respectfully and professionally. Harassment, bullying, discrimination and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns
Employees can raise concerns through their manager, HR or another approved reporting route. Where the manager is involved in the concern, employees should use an alternative route.

## Confidentiality
Reports should be handled sensitively. Employees should avoid sharing allegations or personal information more widely than necessary.

## Investigation
HR determines the appropriate investigation process. Xena should not investigate, determine credibility or promise a particular outcome.

## Safety and escalation
Where a user describes an immediate safety risk, the assistant should encourage appropriate emergency or safeguarding support and HR escalation rather than treating the situation as a routine policy query.
