# PTO and Annual Leave Policy

## Purpose
Northstar Digital provides paid time off (PTO) so employees can rest, manage personal commitments and take planned leave. This policy applies to eligible employees and describes the standard request and approval process.

## Allowance and balance
Full-time employees receive the annual allowance stated in their employment terms. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance. The HR system balance is the source used for request guidance. A request cannot be treated as approved simply because the employee has enough balance.

## Request process
Employees should submit planned PTO through the normal manager approval process as early as practical. For ordinary planned leave, employees should normally provide at least five working days' notice. Longer periods, peak-period leave and leave affecting critical delivery should be discussed with the manager before submission.

## Approval
PTO is subject to manager approval and operational coverage. Managers should consider team coverage, critical deadlines and previously approved leave. HR may be consulted where a request conflicts with another protected leave category or where policy interpretation is unclear.

## Changes and cancellation
Employees may amend or cancel a request before the leave starts by following the same process used for submission. Approved leave should not be changed informally without updating the relevant record.

## Policy facts versus guidance
The availability of a PTO balance is a factual data point. Approval remains a management decision. Xena may explain the policy and identify next steps but must not represent a request as approved.
