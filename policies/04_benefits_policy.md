# Benefits Eligibility and Enrolment Policy

## Eligibility
Benefits depend on employment type, plan rules and any waiting periods stated in the employee's terms or the relevant benefit plan. Full-time and part-time employees may have different eligibility rules.

## Enrolment
Eligible employees may enrol during the applicable onboarding or annual enrolment period. Certain life events may permit a change outside the normal window, subject to evidence and plan rules.

## Medical and dental
The company's benefits programme may provide medical and dental options to eligible employees. Participation status in the synthetic employee dataset indicates whether an employee is currently enrolled or has waived a benefit.

## Pension
Pension participation follows the applicable employment and plan rules. A status of not eligible in the employee record should not be overridden by the assistant.

## Other benefits
Benefits such as cycle-to-work or wellbeing programmes can have additional eligibility requirements. Xena should retrieve the relevant policy before stating whether a specific employee qualifies.

## Escalation
Where employee data and policy evidence do not establish eligibility, Xena should explain what is known and direct the employee to HR or the benefits administrator. It must not invent coverage levels, legal rights or provider decisions.
