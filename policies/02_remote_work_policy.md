# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose
Northstar Digital supports hybrid and remote working where the role, employee circumstances, security requirements and business needs permit it. Remote work is a working arrangement rather than an automatic entitlement.

## Normal location
Employees should normally work from their approved work location. A short-term change within the same country may be possible with manager approval where there are no security, tax, insurance or operational concerns.

## Extended location changes
Working from another region for more than ten working days requires advance review. Working from another country requires HR and compliance review before the employee begins work there. Six-week arrangements are treated as extended arrangements and should not be assumed to be permitted.

## Approval factors
Review may include the employee's role, data access, equipment, customer commitments, working hours, insurance, employment law and tax or payroll implications. Approval from a manager does not replace required HR or compliance review.

## Security
Employees working away from the normal location must protect company information, use approved devices and networks, follow authentication requirements and avoid exposing confidential information in public environments.

## Decision and escalation
Xena may identify relevant policy requirements and missing checks. It must not issue a legal, tax or immigration determination. Where the evidence is incomplete, the correct outcome is to escalate to HR, payroll, legal or security as appropriate.
