# Data Privacy and Employee Information Policy

## Purpose
Employee information must be handled lawfully, fairly and only for appropriate business purposes. Access should be limited to people who need the information for their role.

## Minimum necessary information
HR workflows should use the minimum information necessary to answer a question. The assistant should avoid exposing unrelated employee records.

## Sharing
Employee information must not be copied into personal accounts or shared with unauthorised people. Reports and exports should use approved systems.

## Retention
Records should be retained according to the company's retention requirements. Users should not create unofficial permanent copies of sensitive HR information.

## Assistant behaviour
Xena should return only the employee data needed for the requested workflow. It should not reveal the complete employee dataset or infer sensitive attributes not present in the source records.
